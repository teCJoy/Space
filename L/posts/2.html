1. preferable /ˈprefərəbl/
中释：更可取的，更好的
英释：better or more suitable, especially when compared with something else.

例句：

  • For heavy video editing, a Mac Studio with maxed-out RAM is preferable to any laptop — thermal throttling is a nightmare on portables.
  • Writing code in a dark-themed IDE is preferable to staring at a white screen for eight hours — my eyes thank me.
  • Given the choice between a cheap SSD and a reliable one, reliability is always preferable, even if it costs more.

写作场景：
读后续写：在人物面临选择时，表达某种偏好或权衡。

Staying in the burning building was unthinkable, but jumping from the third floor wasn't preferable either. He had to find a third way.

应用文（建议信）：

For your first DIY PC build, a modular power supply is preferable to a non-modular one — it'll save you tons of cable management headaches.

联想：prefer (v.) → preference (n.) → preferably (adv.)；搭配：be preferable to (比…更可取)

2. consistent /kənˈsɪstənt/
中释：一致的，始终如一的
英释：always behaving or happening in the same way; unchanging.

例句：

  • Apple's design language has been remarkably consistent across product lines — you can always tell a Mac from a PC.
  • My Wi-Fi speed isn't consistent at all — one minute it's 500 Mbps, the next it drops to 20 for no reason.
  • She's been consistent in her practice routine, which explains why her coding skills improved so fast.

写作场景：
读后续写：描写人物的性格稳定或某种状态持续不变。

His kindness was as consistent as the sunrise — she'd never met anyone so reliably good.

应用文（推荐信/陈述）：

He has demonstrated consistent academic excellence throughout high school, ranking in the top 5% every semester.

联想：consistently (adv.) → consistency (n.) → inconsistent (反)；搭配：be consistent with / consistent effort

3. approximate /əˈprɒksɪmət/
中释：近似的，大约的
英释：almost correct or accurate, but not completely; roughly estimated.

例句：

  • The approximate cost of building a decent gaming rig right now is around $1500, but GPU prices keep fluctuating.
  • I asked Siri for the approximate time of sunset, and she gave me a number that was off by 20 minutes.
  • The approximate number of lines in this codebase is 50,000 — it's going to take weeks to refactor.

写作场景：
读后续写：表示粗略的估计或不精确的数据，常用于铺垫紧张或不确定感。

The approximate distance to the nearest town was fifty miles — too far to walk, especially with night falling.

应用文（通知/报告）：

The approximate duration of the maintenance is two hours; please save your work before 10 AM.

联想：approximately (adv.) → approximation (n.)；搭配：approximate value / approximate estimate

4. sensible /ˈsensəbl/
中释：明智的，合理的
英释：based on good judgment and practical ideas; reasonable.

例句：

  • It's sensible to back up your data before installing a beta version of macOS — I learned that after losing a week's work.
  • A sensible overclocking strategy is to increase voltage in tiny steps, not max it out on the first go.
  • She made the sensible decision to buy AppleCare for her new MBP; repairing that display out of pocket is brutal.

写作场景：
读后续写：人物在紧急或压力下做出的合理选择。

It was the only sensible thing to do — leave the burning car behind and run for the tree line.

应用文（建议信）：

My advice is simple: make a sensible budget for your study abroad and stick to it. Unexpected expenses will come up.

联想：sense (n.) → sensibly (adv.) → sensibility (n.)；反义词：silly, foolish；搭配：a sensible decision / sensible advice

5. central /ˈsentrəl/
中释：中心的，主要的
英释：in the middle of something; main or most important.

例句：

  • The CPU is the central component of any computer — everything else is just peripheral.
  • In the Apple ecosystem, iCloud plays a central role in syncing your data across devices.
  • The central issue with this project isn't the budget — it's the lack of clear communication between teams.

写作场景：
读后续写：揭示故事核心矛盾或人物内心核心动机。

The central truth she'd been avoiding was that she didn't want to leave — not really.

应用文（陈述/演讲）：

The central theme of my proposal is sustainability — we need to think about the long-term impact of our actions.

联想：center (n.) → centralize (v.) → centrally (adv.)；搭配：play a central role / central location

6. comparative /kəmˈpærətɪv/
中释：比较的，相对的
英释：measured or judged by comparing with something else; relative.

例句：

  • In comparative terms, Intel's new chips are better than last gen, but they still can't beat Apple's efficiency.
  • The comparative quiet of the library was a welcome relief after the chaos of the lunch hall.
  • She's studying comparative literature, which basically means she reads books from different cultures and compares how they tell the same stories.

写作场景：
读后续写：表达相对程度或对比带来的变化。

After weeks of relentless rain, the comparative warmth of the sun felt like a gift.

应用文（分析/论证）：

The comparative advantage of our plan is that it requires fewer resources while delivering similar results.

联想：compare (v.) → comparison (n.) → comparatively (adv.)；搭配：comparative advantage / in comparative terms

7. applicable /əˈplɪkəbl/
中释：适用的，适当的
英释：relevant to something or able to be used in a particular situation.

例句：

  • This warranty is only applicable if you registered your product within 30 days of purchase.
  • The same troubleshooting steps are applicable to most Android phones, regardless of brand.
  • The rules of good writing are applicable across all genres — clarity, concision, and voice matter everywhere.

写作场景：
读后续写：说明某个规则、原则或方法在特定情境下的可用性。

The strategy he'd used in chess wasn't applicable to real life — people didn't move in predictable patterns.

应用文（通知/说明）：

The discount is applicable to all students with a valid ID card. Please present it at checkout.

联想：apply (v.) → application (n.) → applicability (n.)；搭配：be applicable to / universally applicable

8. complicated /ˈkɒmplɪkeɪtɪd/
中释：复杂的，难懂的
英释：difficult to understand or deal with; having many parts that are connected in a complex way.

例句：

  • The process of dual-booting Windows and Linux on a Mac is complicated enough that most people just give up.
  • His explanation of why the app crashed was so complicated I just nodded and pretended to understand.
  • The relationship between memory speed and CPU performance is more complicated than "faster is better" — it depends on your workload.

写作场景：
读后续写：描写局面、情感或问题的错综复杂。

The truth was far more complicated than she'd imagined — simple answers didn't exist in this story.

应用文（解释/说明）：

The installation process might look complicated, but if you follow the numbered steps, it's actually manageable.

联想：complicate (v.) → complication (n.) → complicatedly (adv.)；反义词：simple；搭配：complicated process / get complicated

9. inevitable /ɪnˈevɪtəbl/
中释：不可避免的
英释：certain to happen and impossible to prevent.

例句：

  • Apple's annual September event makes the inevitable flood of "leaks" and "rumors" almost entertaining.
  • When you build your own PC, the inevitable question from friends is: "Can you build one for me too?"
  • The inevitable crash came after he ignored the low battery warning and kept gaming.

写作场景：
读后续写：营造宿命感或无法回避的结局。

The farewell was inevitable, yet he still felt unprepared when the moment finally arrived.

应用文（分析/预测）：

It seems inevitable that renewable energy will replace fossil fuels in the long run — the question is how fast.

联想：inevitably (adv.) → inevitability (n.)；搭配：inevitable consequence / almost inevitable

10. vague /veɪɡ/
中释：模糊的，含糊的，不明确的
英释：not clear, definite, or detailed; not clearly expressed or perceived.

例句：

  • The error log gave only a vague description — "something went wrong" — which is about as helpful as a broken screen.
  • I have a vague memory of setting up this router, but I definitely forgot the admin password.
  • Her answer was deliberately vague, which made me suspect she knew more than she was letting on.

写作场景：
读后续写：人物回避真相或记忆模糊时的状态。

The face in the photo was vague, almost blurred — but the eyes, those he remembered clearly.

应用文（邮件/反馈）：

Please avoid vague feedback like "it's not good enough." Give specific examples so we can actually improve.

联想：vaguely (adv.) → vagueness (n.)；搭配：vague idea / vague memory / deliberately vague

11. accurate /ˈækjərət/
中释：准确的，精确的
英释：correct and without any mistakes; precise.

例句：

  • The GPS on my phone is accurate to within three meters — good enough for driving, useless for finding a lost AirTag.

  • His assessment of the situation was frighteningly accurate — he predicted every move before it happened.

写作场景：
读后续写：描写人物敏锐的观察力或精准的判断。

Her guess turned out to be eerily accurate — she'd known exactly what he was planning all along.

应用文（说明/报告）：

Please provide an accurate timeline of your project milestones so we can coordinate the resources accordingly.

联想：accuracy (n.) → accurately (adv.) → inaccurate (反)；搭配：accurate information / accurate measurement

12. sudden /ˈsʌdn/
中释：突然的，意外的
英释：happening quickly and unexpectedly without any warning.

例句：

  • A sudden kernel panic — then my unsaved project was gone. Now I compulsively hit Cmd+S every five seconds.
  • The sudden drop in GPU prices made everyone regret buying during the shortage.
  • His sudden interest in coding came out of nowhere — last month he was all about guitar.

写作场景：
读后续写：制造意外转折或人物心理冲击。

The sudden silence after the explosion was more terrifying than the blast itself.

应用文（请假/通知）：

Due to a sudden family emergency, I need to leave early today. I'll catch up on work from home later.

联想：suddenly (adv.) → suddenness (n.)；搭配：all of a sudden / sudden change / sudden death

例句：

14. remarkable /rɪˈmɑːkəbl/
中释：非凡的，显著的
英释：unusual or striking in a way that deserves attention; extraordinary.

例句：

  • The battery life on the M2 MacBook Air is remarkable — I went two days without charging and still had 20% left.
  • She's made remarkable progress in Python in just three months — from "hello world" to building a functional web scraper.
  • The way he recovered from that hard drive failure was remarkable — he had backups everywhere.

写作场景：
读后续写：强调人物成就、变化或某个令人惊叹的瞬间。

He has shown a remarkable ability to solve complex problems calmly and creatively — a rare talent.

联想：remark (v.) → remarkably (adv.) → remarkableness (n.)；搭配：remarkable achievement / remarkable progress

15. probable /ˈprɒbəbl/
中释：很可能的，大概的
英释：likely to happen or be true; probable.

例句：

  • It's highly probable that Apple will announce the M4 at WWDC, but don't expect it to ship until fall.
  • The most probable cause of the system crash is a memory leak in the latest driver update.
  • Given his track record, it's probable he'll finish the project ahead of schedule again.

写作场景：
读后续写：基于现有线索做出的合理推断，常用于推进情节。

The most probable explanation was that she'd taken the wrong turn — but that didn't account for the footprints leading away.

应用文（分析/预测）：

It's probable that the meeting will be postponed due to the speaker's flight delay. I'll keep you posted.

联想：probably (adv.) → probability (n.) → improbable (反)；搭配：highly probable / probable
