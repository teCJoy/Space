#1

1. reluctant /rɪˈlʌktənt/
中释：不情愿的，勉强的
英释：unwilling to do something and therefore slow to do it.

例句：

  • I was reluctant to update to the new iOS because every time Apple "fixes" something, they break three other things.
  • He gave a reluctant nod when I asked to borrow his mechanical keyboard — that thing costs more than my whole monitor.
  • She was reluctant to admit she hadn't understood the concept, so she spent the whole night teaching herself.

写作场景：
读后续写：表现人物内心矛盾——想做但不敢，或不想做但不得不。

He gave a reluctant smile and handed over the keys, knowing he might never see his car again.

应用文（建议信）：

I was reluctant to suggest this at first, but have you considered taking a gap year to figure things out?

联想：reluctantly (adv.) → reluctance (n.)；搭配：be reluctant to do / a reluctant agreement

2. sophisticated /səˈfɪstɪkeɪtɪd/
中释：复杂的，精密的；老练的，有见识的
英释：highly developed and complex; having a lot of experience and knowledge of the world.

例句：

  • The M3's sophisticated power management is why MacBooks run all day while Intel chips still guzzle juice.
  • Building a custom water-cooling loop requires sophisticated planning — you can't just slap tubes together and hope.
  • She has a sophisticated understanding of social dynamics, which makes her an excellent negotiator.

写作场景：
读后续写：写人物“城府深”或某物“设计精巧”。

His answer was too sophisticated for a child — and that's when she realized he wasn't just a kid.

应用文（推荐信）：

She possesses a sophisticated grasp of programming concepts that is rare among students her age.

联想：sophisticatedly (adv.) → sophistication (n.)；搭配：sophisticated technology / sophisticated taste

3. temporary /ˈtemprəri/
中释：暂时的，临时的
英释：lasting for only a limited period of time; not permanent.

例句：

  • Using a cheap USB Wi-Fi dongle is just a temporary fix until I can run Ethernet through the wall.
  • I'm on a temporary contract, so my access to the server room is limited — they don't trust me yet.
  • Most of our worries are temporary — but in the moment, they feel like forever.

写作场景：
读后续写：以为只是暂时的，结果却改变了人生——制造转折。

The job was supposed to be temporary, just something to pay the bills. Three years later, he was still there.

应用文（请假邮件）：

I'm requesting a temporary leave of absence due to family matters. I expect to return by the 20th.

联想：temporarily (adv.) → temporary (反义词 permanent)；搭配：temporary solution / temporary job

4. inevitable /ɪnˈevɪtəbl/
中释：不可避免的
英释：certain to happen and impossible to avoid.

例句：

  • Battery degradation is inevitable on any phone — but Apple makes it annoyingly hard to replace yourself.
  • Once you start building your own PCs, upgrading every year becomes inevitable. It's an addiction, honestly.
  • The inevitable question came: "Why are you still single?" and she gave her usual shrug.

写作场景：
读后续写：铺垫命运感或无力感。

The goodbye was inevitable. They'd known it since the day they met, but knowing didn't make it easier.

应用文（分析/报告）：

Given the current trend, it seems inevitable that online learning will play a larger role in education.

联想：inevitably (adv.) → inevitability (n.)；搭配：inevitable consequence / seemingly inevitable

5. delicate /ˈdelɪkət/
中释：精巧的，纤细的；微妙的，需要谨慎处理的
英释：easily broken or damaged; requiring careful handling; sensitive.

例句：

  • The ribbon cable inside a laptop is incredibly delicate — one wrong move and you've killed the keyboard.
  • Installing a CPU is delicate work. Line up the triangle, drop it in, and don't sneeze.
  • She has a delicate way of saying harsh truths without offending anyone.

写作场景：
读后续写：一触即碎的关系、物品或情绪状态。

She held the old photograph with delicate fingers, as if it might turn to dust if she gripped too hard.

应用文（邮件/建议）：

This is a delicate matter, so please handle the conversation with care. I trust your judgment.

联想：delicately (adv.) → delicacy (n.)；搭配：delicate balance / a delicate situation

6. dull /dʌl/
中释：乏味的，无聊的；钝的，不锋利的
英释：not interesting or exciting; not sharp.

例句：

  • A dull knife in the kitchen is bad, but a dull drill bit is worse — you'll crack your case before you make a hole.
  • That monitor has such dull colors I thought it was broken — turns out it's just a cheap panel.
  • His presentation was so dull that half the class was checking their watches.

写作场景：
读后续写：平淡生活中出现打破节奏的事件。

It was a dull afternoon, the kind where nothing happens — until the doorbell rang.

应用文（建议）：

Try to avoid dull, generic phrases in your college essay. Admissions officers read thousands of them.

联想：dully (adv.) → dullness (n.)；搭配：dull routine / a dull sound / dull pain

7. fierce /fɪəs/
中释：凶猛的，激烈的，猛烈的
英释：violent, strong, or aggressive; showing a strong and determined attitude.

例句：

  • The competition in the GPU market is fierce right now — Intel, AMD, and Nvidia are all fighting for every dollar.
  • The fans on my gaming laptop kicked in with a fierce roar as soon as I launched Cyberpunk.
  • She has a fierce determination that scares some people and inspires others.

写作场景：
读后续写：人物“不服输”的性格或自然环境的猛烈。

There was a fierce gleam in his eyes — the kind that said he'd rather die than give up.

应用文（陈述/推荐）：

I'm a fierce advocate for student-led initiatives, and I believe this project deserves school funding.

联想：fiercely (adv.) → fierceness (n.)；搭配：fierce competition / fierce loyalty

8. hostile /ˈhɒstaɪl/
中释：敌对的，敌意的；恶劣的，不利的
英释：unfriendly and aggressive; showing dislike or anger.

例句：

  • The bootloader on this device is hostile to third-party OSes — they've locked it down tight.
  • The hostile environment inside a gaming PC case — dust, heat, vibration — kills cheap fans within months.
  • The audience wasn't just cold — they were openly hostile. He walked off stage humiliated.

写作场景：
读后续写：描写“敌意”的氛围或对抗性关系。

The silence in the room wasn't peaceful — it was hostile, like everyone was holding their breath before a fight.

应用文（投诉/反馈）：

I received a hostile response to my suggestion, which I found unprofessional and discouraging.

联想：hostilely (adv.) → hostility (n.)；搭配：hostile environment / a hostile crowd

9. inferior /ɪnˈfɪəriə/
中释：低等的，较差的，劣质的
英释：not as good as something else; lower in rank or quality.

例句：

  • TN panels are inferior to IPS in every way except response time — and that gap is closing.
  • Using a cheap power supply isn't just "inferior" — it's dangerous. I've seen them catch fire.
  • He always felt inferior to his older brother, who seemed to succeed at everything effortlessly.

写作场景：
读后续写：刻画人物心理——自卑、不甘、想要证明自己。

He was tired of feeling inferior. That's why he studied until 2 AM every night, determined to outshine them all.

应用文（建议）：

Don't buy inferior equipment just because it's cheap — it'll cost you more in repairs later.

联想：inferiority (n.)；反义词 superior；搭配：inferior quality / feel inferior to

10. rare /reə/
中释：稀有的，罕见的，不常发生的
英释：not common or frequent; very unusual.

例句：

  • Finding a perfectly working iPod Classic is rare — and when you do, people want a fortune for it.
  • It's rare for a phone to last more than three years without some battery or port issue.
  • It's rare to find someone who truly listens instead of just waiting for their turn to speak.

写作场景：
读后续写：描写“罕见的时刻”或“珍贵的机会”。

It was a rare moment of honesty between them — the kind that changes everything or breaks it.

应用文（邀请/推荐）：

This is a rare opportunity to learn directly from a Nobel laureate — I strongly encourage you to attend.

联想：rarely (adv.) → rarity (n.)；搭配：rare opportunity / a rare species

11. superficial /ˌsuːpəˈfɪʃl/
中释：表面的，肤浅的
英释：existing or appearing at the surface only; not deep or thorough.

例句：

  • Most hardware reviews are superficial — they just benchmark and move on without testing real-world use.
  • The fix looked superficial — replacing the screen — but the logic board damage was the real issue.
  • Social media gives us a superficial view of people's lives, and we compare our behind-the-scenes to their highlight reels.

写作场景：
读后续写：“看起来如此，实际并非如此”的转折。

His apology felt superficial, like he'd rehearsed it in the mirror. She wasn't buying it.

应用文（分析/陈述）：

I don't want to give a superficial answer to this question — I'd like to explore it in more depth.

联想：superficially (adv.) → superficiality (n.)；搭配：superficial wound / superficial understanding

12. tremendous /trəˈmendəs/
中释：极大的，巨大的，惊人的
英释：very great in amount, size, or degree; extraordinary.

例句：

  • The performance jump from the M1 to the M3 was tremendous — I thought my render was broken because it finished too fast.
  • The amount of heat generated by a 4090 is tremendous — you could heat a small room with it.
  • The support from my friends made a tremendous difference during that difficult year.

写作场景：
读后续写：烘托“震撼感”——规模、力量或情感上的巨大。

The sound was tremendous — like the sky itself was tearing apart. They covered their ears and ran.

应用文（感谢信/推荐）：

I want to express my tremendous gratitude to everyone who helped organize this event.

联想：tremendously (adv.)；搭配：tremendous impact / tremendous effort

13. vague /veɪɡ/
中释：模糊的，含糊的，不明确的
英释：not clear or definite; not clearly expressed or understood.

例句：

  • The error message was so vague I had no idea what went wrong — just "something failed." Thanks, very helpful.
  • Apple's official statement on the battery issue was intentionally vague, which made everyone more suspicious.
  • I have a vague memory of meeting him before, but I can't place where.

写作场景：
读后续写：人物“回避”或“隐瞒”时的状态。

Her answer was deliberately vague, and he knew she was hiding something. The question was: how far would he push?

应用文（邮件/通知）：

Please avoid vague language in your application. Admissions officers want clarity, not guesswork.

联想：vaguely (adv.) → vagueness (n.)；搭配：a vague idea / vague memory

14. vivid /ˈvɪvɪd/
中释：生动的，鲜明的，逼真的
英释：producing strong, clear images in the mind; bright and distinct.

例句：

  • The OLED screen on this phone delivers such vivid colors that LCD looks washed out by comparison.
  • I still have a vivid memory of installing my first SSD — the boot time went from 2 minutes to 15 seconds.
  • He has a vivid imagination — he can turn a boring bus ride into a full adventure story.

写作场景：
读后续写：描写“画面感强的记忆或场景”——让读者身临其境。

Twenty years later, he could still recall the scene with vivid clarity: the red umbrella, the rain, her laugh.

应用文（推荐/分享）：

The author's vivid storytelling makes this book a pleasure to read — I'd highly recommend it.

联想：vividly (adv.) → vividness (n.)；搭配：vivid memory / vivid imagination

15. sheer /ʃɪə/
中释：完全的，纯粹的；陡峭的，垂直的
英释：nothing other than; complete; very steep.

例句：

  • The sheer complexity of modern CPUs is mind-blowing — billions of transistors in a chip smaller than your fingernail.
  • The sheer size of the codebase made me want to just close the project and walk away.
  • He stared at the sheer cliff face and wondered how anyone could possibly climb it.

写作场景：
读后续写：强调某种纯粹到极致的感受——恐惧、喜悦、数量、难度。

She was frozen by the sheer terror of the moment — her feet wouldn't move, her voice wouldn't come out.

应用文（陈述/推荐）：

The sheer number of applicants this year makes it harder than ever to stand out. Focus on your unique strengths.

联想：搭配：sheer luck / sheer size / sheer volume
